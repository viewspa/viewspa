What's really going on

When your body holds extra fluid

Your lymphatic system is how the body clears excess fluid and waste from the tissues - but unlike blood, lymph has no pump. It relies entirely on movement and muscle activity to keep flowing. When it slows down, fluid pools: you feel puffy, heavy, swollen around the ankles or midsection, and generally sluggish.

You notice it in small, specific ways. The ring that goes on fine in the morning and not by evening. The shoes you now buy half a size up. The photos where your face looks heavier than you feel. Nothing dramatic - just a body that seems to be holding onto something.

It's common after long flights or a lot of sitting, in hot Florida weather, during hormonal shifts, and after certain surgeries or cosmetic procedures where the body needs help moving fluid out of an area. Lymphatic drainage massage is the technique designed specifically to get that flow moving again - gently, and in the direction your body already wants it to go.

Why a regular massage works differently

Deep tissue and sports massage press firmly into muscle - the exact opposite of what lymph needs. The lymphatic vessels sit just under the skin, so the technique is light, slow and rhythmic, following the direction lymph naturally drains toward the nodes. Done well it feels gentle and calming rather than deep, and that lightness is precisely what makes it effective. It's a specific, directional method - not a softer version of an ordinary massage.

How the session is structured

Ivan begins by understanding why you're seeking drainage - post-travel puffiness, general fluid retention, recovery after a procedure, or simply feeling heavy and wanting a reset. He then works with light, rhythmic strokes that follow your lymphatic pathways, typically starting near the major nodes to open the drainage routes before moving to the areas holding fluid. The pace stays slow and steady throughout - this technique is never rushed. If you're recovering from a specific procedure, tell Ivan in advance so he can work appropriately and confirm you're cleared for it.

What to expect after

Many people feel lighter and less puffy soon after, and it's normal to visit the bathroom more over the following day as fluid moves through - that's exactly the point. Drinking water afterward supports the process. The session itself is calming, and a lot of clients find it deeply relaxing. Ivan may suggest movement or a follow-up rhythm depending on your goal.

How many sessions you'll need

For a one-off reset - after travel or a heavy week - a single session can leave you feeling noticeably lighter. For ongoing fluid retention or post-procedure recovery, a short series usually gives better, longer-lasting results, because the effect builds with regular sessions. Ivan recommends a realistic number based on what you're working with, and never more than you need. The 4-pack ($300) and 8-pack ($590) can be used for a drainage series if that fits your goal.
