Why it matters

What you can actually catch

A poorly cleaned tool can pass fungus, bacterial infections, and in the worst case bloodborne viruses like hepatitis. All it takes is a tiny nick by the cuticle you won't even feel. This isn't paranoia — it's the exact reason serious studios put sterilization above looks.

Why the average salon leaves you guessing

Often a tool is just wiped with alcohol or dropped in solution — not enough to kill everything. And you can't see what happened to it before you: whose hands it just came out of, or how it was cleaned. "Probably clean" isn't a guarantee. It's a hope.

How sterility works here

Metal tools are sterilized in an autoclave — the same standard used in surgery. Every set is sealed in its own pouch and opened in front of you at the start of the appointment. Files and buffers are single-use — new ones out of the pack, in your sight. The table and surfaces are disinfected before and after. You don't have to take it on faith — you watch it happen.

What you'll see at your appointment
