Cancellation & No-Show Policy

Plans change - we get it. All we ask is a day's notice, so the time can go to someone else. Here's exactly how it works.

Effective August 2026

The short version

Cancel or reschedule free - anytime up to 24 hours before your appointment.

Less than 24 hours - 50% of the service price.

No-show - 100% of the service price.

Running late? You have a 15-minute grace period - just text us.

That's the whole policy. The rest of this page explains the details and the why.

Why we have it

Every appointment is booked with one specialist who holds that time only for you. It's a private studio with two chairs - there's no back room and no one to fill in.

When someone cancels an hour before, that time can't be given to anyone else. The specialist loses the income, and another client who wanted that slot never got the chance to take it.

A day's notice fixes both. That's all this policy is for.

The 24-hour window

You can cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before your appointment at no charge, for any reason. No explanation needed.

Example. Your appointment is Tuesday at 2:00 PM.

Cancel any time before Monday 2:00 PM - free.

Cancel after Monday 2:00 PM - the late cancellation fee applies.

Rescheduling counts the same as cancelling: move your appointment with a day's notice and there's no charge.

Fees

Late cancellation - 50%

Cancelling or rescheduling less than 24 hours before your appointment is 50% of the service price.

No-show - 100%

Not arriving, without letting us know, is the full price of the service. Your specialist held that time and turned other clients away for it.

How fees are handled

We don't keep your card on file, and nothing is charged automatically. Booking online costs you nothing up front - you pay at your appointment, as always.

If a fee applies, we'll tell you what happened and why. It's then settled before your next appointment can be confirmed - either paid directly or prepaid together with your next booking.

How to cancel or reschedule

Any of these, any time of day - the 24-hour clock is what matters, not our opening hours:

Call (754) 202-6666 Text us on WhatsApp

You can also email viewspa.fl@gmail.com, or use the cancel link in your confirmation email. We confirm every cancellation back to you - if you didn't get a reply, assume it didn't reach us and call.

Running late

You have a 15-minute grace period. Past that, the appointment is treated as a no-show and the time is released.

If you're running behind, text us as early as you can. Usually one of these happens:

  • We still fit you in as booked;
  • Your specialist does a shortened version of the service in the time that's left;
  • We move you to another slot - free, if it's more than 24 hours away.

Emergencies

Real life happens - illness, accidents, a family emergency. Tell us as soon as you know, and we'll work it out. We waive fees for genuine emergencies, and we don't ask for proof.

What isn't an emergency: "I forgot" or "something came up." We're not trying to be harsh about it - that's simply the situation this policy exists for.

Repeat no-shows

Almost nobody reads this far, because almost nobody needs it. But to be straight with you:

  • After two no-shows in six months, we ask for prepayment to hold a slot, and the notice window becomes 72 hours.
  • After three, we can only take you same-day, when a slot happens to be free.

Six months with no missed appointments and the record clears. And if scheduling is genuinely hard for you - tell us. We'd rather find a time that works than count strikes.

If we cancel on you

Rare, but it happens - illness on our side, mostly. If it does:

  • You're never charged anything, whatever the timing;
  • You get first pick of the next available slots;
  • If we made you cancel something else, we'll make it right.

Gift cards and prepaid packages

  • Reschedule with a day's notice - nothing is lost, your balance stays as it is.
  • Late cancellation or no-show - the fee comes out of the balance, same percentages as above.
  • Gift cards don't expire.

Straight answers

  • Is this strict? The 24-hour window is standard for appointment-based studios. What's generous here is that we ask for nothing up front.
  • Do we apply it to everyone? Yes - regulars included. It stops being fair the moment it's selective.
  • Do we profit from fees? No. A fee never covers what an empty chair costs. We'd always rather you came.
  • Will we make exceptions? For real emergencies, yes. Not as a loophole.

One thing that helps

We send a reminder before your appointment. That's your cue to confirm, move it, or cancel while it's still free. If something's off, that reminder is the moment to tell us.

Booking an appointment at View Spa means you accept this policy. Questions about it? Call or text - we'll explain anything here.
