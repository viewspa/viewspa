Privacy Policy

We are a two-person studio, not a data company. This page says plainly what we collect, why, and who else sees it. No boilerplate - everything listed here is something our site or our booking system actually does.

Effective September 2026

The short version

We collect what we need to book you - your name, phone, and email if you give it.

We do not sell your data. Not to anyone, ever.

We do not send marketing you did not ask for - and every message we do send has a way to stop it.

You can ask us to delete your data - write to viewspa.fl@gmail.com and we will.

What we collect when you book

To make an appointment we ask for your name and phone number. Email is optional - if you give it, that is where your confirmation and reminder go.

For massage appointments there is a free-text box asking what is bothering you. You choose what to write there. It goes to your therapist so the session is useful rather than generic - nothing more.

Your booking is stored in Square, the appointment and payment system we use. Square holds your contact details and visit history under its own privacy policy.

What we collect when you just visit

Like most sites, ours uses a few tools that set cookies or store small amounts of data in your browser. Here is the complete list:

WhatWhyWho runs it
Google Analytics How many people visit and which pages they read. We look at totals, not at individuals. Google
Google Ads tag Only active if you arrived by clicking one of our ads - it tells us the ad worked. Google
Cloudflare Turnstile Keeps bots from flooding our booking form. It does not track you around the web. Cloudflare
Square Runs on the gift card page so payments can be taken securely. Square
Our own storage Remembers how you found us, and fills your name back in if you return to the booking form. Stays in your browser. View Spa

We do not use Facebook or Instagram tracking pixels, and we do not run retargeting ads that follow you to other sites.

Card payments

If you buy a gift card or a package, your card details go straight to Square from your browser. They never pass through our systems and we never see or store them. We receive only confirmation that the payment succeeded.

Emails we send

After your visit we may send one short email asking how it went. If you have not been in for a while we may send one invitation to come back - at most once every six months.

Every such email has a way to stop it: reply with the word STOP, or just reply and say so. We will not write again. Confirmations of things you paid for - a gift card code, a package - still reach you, because you are owed them.

Appointment confirmations and reminders come from Square, not from us.

Who else sees your data

Only the services that make the studio work, and only the part each one needs:

  • Square - appointments, payments, your contact details
  • Resend - delivers the emails described above
  • Cloudflare - runs our booking system and protects the form
  • Google - site statistics and, when we advertise, ad measurement

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We have never done it and have no plans to.

How long we keep it

Appointment and purchase records stay as long as we are in business - we need them for accounting and to know your history when you come back. Site statistics are kept for 14 months and then deleted by Google automatically.

Ask us to delete your records and we will, except where we are legally required to keep a payment record.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to viewspa.fl@gmail.com and we will answer.

You can also block cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work - you may just have to type your name into the booking form again.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Minors are welcome in the studio with a parent or guardian present, who makes the booking.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will change this page and update the date at the top. We will not quietly start doing something this page says we do not do.

Questions

Ask us directly - we answer.
